# Luân Hồi Online — Lore, Narrative & Cultural Research Document

## 1. Cultural & Religious Framework: Authentic Distinction Matrix

**Luân Hồi Online** blends ethical reflection, peaceful contemplation, and dramatic realm progression across three cosmic planes (**Tam Giới**: *Phàm Giới*, *Thiên Đình*, *Âm Phủ*). Because East Asian and Vietnamese popular culture historically interweaves canonical Buddhism, folk religion, Taoist cosmology, and modern *Xianxia* (Tiên Hiệp) literary tropes, this project **explicitly distinguishes** these three layers in both UI annotations (`traditionNote`, `culturalOrigin`) and NPC dialogues.

## 2. World Mythology & The Three Realms (Tam Giới)

### 2.1 Phàm Giới — The Human Realm (Chùa Linh Sơn Cổ Tự)
- **Philosophical Role:** The precious pivot of spiritual practice. In Buddhist cosmology, the human realm (*Manussa-loka*) contains enough suffering (*Dukkha*) to inspire spiritual urgency (*Saṃvega*) and enough freedom and clarity to cultivate wisdom (*Paññā*) and compassion (*Karuṇā*).
- **Visual & Architectural Setting:** Traditional Vietnamese Buddhist temple architecture (*Chùa Việt cổ truyền*) featuring curved tiled roofs with dragon-phoenix motifs (*mái đao đầu rồng*), wooden ironwood pillars (*cột gỗ lim*), a Three-Jewel Gate (*Cổng Tam Quan*), a tranquil Lotus Pond (*Hồ Sen Tịnh Tâm*), a Bronze Bell Tower (*Gác Chuông Đại Hồng Chung*), and a Bodhi Tree courtyard (*Sân Bồ Đề*).
- **Core Activities:** Bowing to the Three Jewels (*Lễ Phật*), seated meditation (*Thiền Định*), counting 108 mala beads (*Lần Chuỗi*), offering incense and lotus flowers (*Dâng Hương, Dâng Sen*), studying the Wisdom Library (*Đọc Kinh / Thư Viện Trí Tuệ*), and resolving ethical dilemmas with villagers and pilgrims.

### 2.2 Thiên Đình — The Celestial Realm (Cửu Trùng Vân Cung)
- **Philosophical Role:** A realm of luminous bliss and elevated spiritual attainment, accessible once a practitioner reaches **Rank Stage 7 (Tiên Nhân — Immortal)** and maintains a pure karmic balance.
- **Cultural Synthesis:** Combines the Buddhist concept of *Deva-loka* (Heavenly Realms where beings enjoy the fruits of wholesome karma but remain subject to impermanence unless they cultivate liberating wisdom) with Xianxia *Thiên Đình* aesthetics (floating jade pavilions, bridges of auspicious clouds, golden lotus platforms, and the **Đài Độ Kiếp** Tribulation Arena).
- **Core Activities:** Co-op Heavenly Tribulations (*Vượt Thiên Kiếp*), celestial group meditation, mentoring lower-realm practitioners, and earning *Tiên Ngọc*.

### 2.3 Âm Phủ — The Underworld & 18 Levels of Hell (U Minh Giới & Thập Bát Địa Ngục)
- **Philosophical Role:** When a player's **Karma Balance** (`merit - negativeKarma`) falls to `-100` or lower due to harmful choices in ethical dilemmas, they are summoned before **Phán Quan Thôi Giác** at the **Underworld Judgment Hall (Điện Phán Xét)** and assigned to one of the **18 Levels of Hell** matching the severity of their negative karma (`-100` at Level 1 down to `-3500` at Level 18).
- **Redemptive Mechanics (No Permanent Lockout):** In authentic Buddhist teaching, *Naraka* (hell) is **never eternal**. When unwholesome karma is exhausted and the mind awakens to remorse (*Hiri-Ottappa* — moral shame and dread of wrongdoing) and right view (*Sammā-diṭṭhi*), the realm dissolves. Every level of hell in **Luân Hồi Online** features a **Redemption Quest (Hành Trình Chuộc Lỗi)** and an interactive contemplation with its Warden (*Ngục Chủ*). Choosing the awakened path restores `karmaBalance` to `0` and returns the player to *Phàm Giới*.

### Detailed Level Breakdown & Redemption Design

#### Tầng 1: Địa Ngục Vọng Ngôn (Labyrinth of Whispered Lies)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-100`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** Pale violet-gray stone corridors of shifting bone-white masks and echoing sound-tubes. Every lie spoken in the human world crystallizes into a phantom wall that blocks the exit.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Thiệt Kính* (Guardian of the Tongue-Mirror).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`truth_labyrinth`):** Whispering echoes repeat distorted rumors. The player must locate three *Chân Ngôn Bi* (Steles of Truthful Speech), confess the nature of false speech, and align with the Fourth Precept (*Chánh Ngữ*).

#### Tầng 2: Địa Ngục Trọng Tham (Mirage Desert of Endless Greed)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-250`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** A vast expanse of parched golden-ash sand filled with sunken treasure pavilions. Illusory coins turn into heavy lead weights that slow movement the more a soul clings to them.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Kim Sa* (Warden of Golden Ash).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`greed_renunciation`):** Players must cast away illusory spectral hoards at three Alms Bowls of Generosity (*Bát Bố Thí*), learning that true wealth lies in contentment (*Tri Túc*).

#### Tầng 3: Địa Ngục Liệt Sân (Volcanic Crater of Wrath)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-400`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** Jagged obsidian bridges spanning rivers of crimson magma. Basalt pillars crack whenever a soul harbors hostility; the air shimmers with embers born from furious words and deeds.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Viêm Nộ* (Warden of the Blazing Hearth).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`anger_quell`):** As taught in *Dhammapada* verse 5, hatred is never appeased by hatred, only by non-hatred (*Avera*). Players channel cooling waters of patience (*Nhẫn Nhục*) at three magma vents.

#### Tầng 4: Địa Ngục Kính Đố Kỵ (Hall of Envious Mirrors)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-550`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** An octagonal palace of tall emerald-tinted bronze mirrors. Those who begrudged others' happiness see only distorted, fractured reflections of their own scarcity.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Tật Đố* (Keeper of the Fractured Glass).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`mirror_reflection`):** Players practice *Tùy Hỷ* (Muditā — sympathetic joy in others' virtues and blessings), polishing three tarnished mirrors until they reflect clear light.

#### Tầng 5: Địa Ngục Vực Bội Tín (Chasm of Broken Vows)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-700`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** Suspended rope-and-slate bridges hanging over an indigo chasm where broken oaths fall like shattered jade tablets. Every step taken in deceit causes the stones beneath to crumble.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Đoạn Thệ* (Guardian of the Severed Oath).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`bridge_of_vows`):** Reconstruct the Bridge of Integrity (*Cầu Tín Nghĩa*) by anchoring three cornerstone vows of trustworthiness and accountability.

#### Tầng 6: Địa Ngục Hàn Vô Tình (Glacier of Callous Indifference)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-900`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** Towering spires of translucent blue-black ice where souls who turned a blind eye to suffering stand frozen in self-isolation.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Hàn Băng* (Warden of the Frozen Heart).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`soul_warmth`):** Ignite three Braziers of Empathy (*Lò Lửa Đồng Cảm*) by sharing spiritual warmth with shivering shades.

#### Tầng 7: Địa Ngục Hỏa Ác Khẩu (Furnace of Venomous Tongues)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-1100`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** Bronze bell-furnaces and iron pillars etched with sharp script. Sparks rain down like barbed arrows—the karmic echo of slander, cyber-bullying, and cruel mockery.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Xích Thiệt* (Warden of the Searing Word).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`mantra_shield`):** Recite and resonate four soothing chimes of Loving Speech (*Ái Ngữ*), transforming searing sparks into cooling lotus rain.

#### Tầng 8: Địa Ngục Mê Đạo Tặc (Shifting Vault of Stolen Karma)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-1300`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** Escher-like interlocking stone vaults and rotating brass locks. Those who took what was not given (*Tà Đạo / Trộm Cắp*) find every stolen prize locking them inside a tighter cage.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Tỏa Linh* (Keeper of the Shifting Locks).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`return_relics`):** Return four displaced karmic relics to their rightful pedestals, honoring the Second Precept (*Không Trộm Cắp*).

#### Tầng 9: Địa Ngục Thiết Bạo (Iron Bastion of Cruelty)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-1500`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** A grim fortress of rusted iron ramparts and heavy chains. The chains are forged not by demons, but by every act of violence and intimidation inflicted upon living beings.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Thiết Xiềng* (Warden of the Iron Shackle).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`unshackle_peace`):** Lay down all aggression and unshackle four bound spirits through the First Precept of Non-Harming (*Bất Sát / Từ Bi*).

#### Tầng 10: Địa Ngục Mộng Ngụy Tạo (Phantasm Theater of Hypocrisy)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-1700`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** An opulent yet hollow theater of gilded paper lanterns, silk curtains, and painted masks. Those who wore the robes of virtue to deceive others wander among lifeless puppets.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Huyễn Diện* (Director of the Painted Mask).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`pierce_illusion`):** Extinguish four false lanterns and remove the mask of pretension, embracing sincere humility (*Trực Tâm*).

#### Tầng 11: Địa Ngục Rừng Vong Ơn (Withered Forest of Ingratitude)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-1900`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** A petrified forest of hollow, leafless trees whose roots are severed from the earth—symbolizing those who forgot their parents, teachers, friends, and homeland (*Tứ Trọng Ân*).
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Khô Mộc* (Keeper of the Severed Root).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`memory_gratitude`):** Water four withered ancestral roots with the Waters of Remembrance (*Nước Nguồn Tri Ân*) until blossoms return.

#### Tầng 12: Địa Ngục Thành Kiêu Mạn (Inverted Spire of Arrogance)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-2100`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** A dizzying upside-down pagoda of cold white marble and gilded stairs that climb into emptiness. The higher one's pride (*Ngã Mạn*), the heavier gravity pulls them into isolation.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Cô Đỉnh* (Warden of the Solitary Peak).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`humility_descent`):** Descend from the pedestal of ego by performing five prostrations of humility (*Khiêm Cung Lễ*).

#### Tầng 13: Địa Ngục Đài Phá Hoại (Shattered Sanctuary of Sabotage)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-2300`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** Floating fragments of broken bridges, toppled stupas, and torn scrolls drifting in a tempest of ash—reflecting those who sowed discord and destroyed communal harmony.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Tàn垣 (Tàn Viên)* (Guardian of the Ruined Sanctuary).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`rebuild_sanctuary`):** Gather five scattered dharma stones to restore the Stupa of Harmony (*Tháp Hòa Hợp*).

#### Tầng 14: Địa Ngục Đầm Dục Vọng (Miasma Mire of Blind Craving)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-2550`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** A purple-black marsh shrouded in intoxicating, suffocating mist (*Ái Dục*). Illusory blossoms lure the senses into quicksand.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Trầm Luân* (Warden of the Sinking Mire).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`lotus_purity`):** Cultivate five white lotus seeds in the mud, realizing that just as the lotus rises unstained from mire, mindfulness transcends blind craving.

#### Tầng 15: Địa Ngục Cân Lạm Quyền (Iron Court of Abused Authority)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-2800`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** A colossal subterranean tribunal dominated by a towering bronze scale and pillars of law. Those who used power, wealth, or status to crush the vulnerable face the exact weight of their decrees.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Thiết Bình* (Magistrate of the Iron Scale).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`scales_of_justice`):** Balance five karmic weights on the Scales of Equity by relinquishing privilege and restoring justice to the wronged.

#### Tầng 16: Địa Ngục Sương Vô Minh (Abyssal Fog of Willful Ignorance)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-3050`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** A boundless labyrinth of dense slate-black fog where compasses spin wildly. Those who willfully denied cause and effect (*Phủ Nhận Nhân Quả*) and misled others wander sightlessly.
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Mê Đồ* (Watcher of the Sightless Fog).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`lamp_of_wisdom`):** Kindle five Lamps of Prajñā (*Đèn Tuệ Giác*) by contemplating Dependent Origination (*Duyên Khởi*).

#### Tầng 17: Địa Ngục Xiềng Chấp Niệm (Shadow Prison of Obsessive Grudges)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-3300`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** Concentric rings of dark iron cages bound by glowing violet chains of memory. Crucially, the cage doors are unlocked from the inside—the prisoners are held only by their refusal to let go of hatred and obsession (*Chấp Niệm*).
- **Warden NPC:** *Ngục Chủ Tâm Lao* (Warden of the Self-Forged Cage).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`sever_attachment`):** Open five inner locks by practicing radical forgiveness and releasing the burden of the past (*Buông Xả*).

#### Tầng 18: Địa Ngục Vô Gián (Avīci — The Uninterrupted Void)
- **Karma Threshold:** `-3500`
- **Visual & Architectural Style:** A vast, silent, dark-crimson and obsidian void where time feels endless (*Vô Gián*). Yet at the very center of the abyss stands a single radiant golden staff—the **Tích Trượng of Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva (Địa Tạng Vương Bồ Tát)**, embodying the vow: *"Địa ngục vị không, thệ bất thành Phật"* ("Until the hells are empty, I vow not to attain Buddhahood").
- **Warden NPC:** *Hóa Thân Địa Tạng Hộ Pháp* (Guardian Manifestation at the Gate of Avīci).
- **Punishment & Redemption Mechanic (`avici_awakening`):** Complete six stages of deep repentance (*Chí Tâm Sám Hối*), awakening the innate Buddha-nature (*Phật Tính*) that cannot be destroyed even in the deepest abyss.

## 4. The Wisdom Library Curriculum (Thư Viện Trí Tuệ — 10 Core Topics)

The **Wisdom Library** is an interactive educational and ethical system. Each of the 10 entries includes:
1. **Authentic Tradition & Scriptural Citation** (never fabricated).
2. **Narrative Story (Câu Chuyện Đạo Lý)** accessible to modern players.
3. **Interactive Moral Dilemma (Tình Huống Nhân Quả)** with three distinct choices:
   - **Compassionate (`compassionate`):** Grants positive **Công Đức (Merit)** and EXP, reinforcing ethical understanding.
   - **Neutral (`neutral`):** Grants modest EXP with minimal karmic shift, representing passive avoidance.
   - **Harmful (`harmful`):** Adds **Nghiệp Lực (`negativeKarma`)**, decreasing `karmaBalance`. This allows players to directly experience how unwholesome intentions accumulate negative karma and—if repeated—trigger **Karma Judgment (Phán Xét Nghiệp Lực)** into the 18 Levels of Hell.
4. **Reflection Question (Câu Hỏi Chiêm Nghiệm)** for personal mindfulness.

### Summary of the 10 Wisdom Categories

1. **`four_noble_truths` — Tứ Diệu Đế: Bốn Sự Thật Cao Quý**
   - *Citation:* *Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta* (SN 56.11 / Tương Ưng Bộ Kinh).
   - *Core Teaching:* Recognizing suffering (*Khổ*), its origin in craving (*Tập*), its cessation (*Diệt*), and the path leading to cessation (*Đạo*).
2. **`eightfold_path` — Bát Chánh Đạo: Con Đường Trung Đạo**
   - *Citation:* *Magga-Vibhaṅga Sutta* (SN 45.8).
   - *Core Teaching:* The eight interconnected practices of Wisdom (*Tuệ*: Right View, Right Intention), Morality (*Giới*: Right Speech, Right Action, Right Livelihood), and Concentration (*Định*: Right Effort, Right Mindfulness, Right Concentration).
3. **`five_precepts` — Ngũ Giới: Năm Nền Tảng Đạo Đức Bảo Hộ Bình An**
   - *Citation:* *Abhisanda Sutta* (AN 8.39 — "Gifts of Peace").
   - *Core Teaching:* Practicing the Five Precepts (not harming life, not stealing, not committing misconduct, not lying, not clouding the mind with intoxicants) as a boundless gift of safety to all beings.
4. **`compassion` — Từ Bi: Tâm Rộng Lượng Thương Yêu Muôn Loài**
   - *Citation:* *Karaniya Mettā Sutta* (Sutta Nipāta 1.8) & *Lotus Sutra* Chapter 25 (*Phẩm Phổ Môn*).
   - *Core Teaching:* *Mettā* (wishing all beings happiness) and *Karuṇā* (acting to alleviate suffering) without discrimination.
5. **`forgiveness` — Bao Dung & Xả Hận: Hóa Giải Oán Thù Bằng Tâm Không Hận**
   - *Citation:* *Dhammapada* Verse 5 (Yamakavagga) & *Kakacūpama Sutta* (MN 21).
   - *Core Teaching:* *"Hatred is never appeased by hatred in this world; by non-hatred alone is hatred appeased. This is an ancient law."*
6. **`mindfulness` — Chánh Niệm: Trở Về Hơi Thở Và Giây Phút Hiện Tại**
   - *Citation:* *Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta* (MN 10 / Kinh Niệm Xứ) & *Bhaddekaratta Sutta* (MN 131).
   - *Core Teaching:* Not dwelling in the past or longing for an unarrived future, but observing body, feelings, mind, and dhammas clearly in the present moment.
7. **`impermanence` — Vô Thường: Mọi Pháp Hữu Vi Đều Biến Đổi**
   - *Citation:* *Mahāparinibbāna Sutta* (DN 16) & *Kisāgotamī Commentary* (DhpA 8.13).
   - *Core Teaching:* Impermanence is not pessimism; because all conditioned things change, suffering can be healed and spiritual growth is possible.
8. **`non_attachment` — Vô Chấp & Buông Xả: Chiếc Bè Qua Sông**
   - *Citation:* *Alagaddūpama Sutta* (MN 22 — Simile of the Raft) & *Heart Sutra* (*Bát Nhã Tâm Kinh*).
   - *Core Teaching:* Clinging turns even helpful things into burdens; letting go brings liberation and lightness of heart.
9. **`karma_causality` — Luật Nhân Quả: Tác Ý Tạo Nghiệp**
   - *Citation:* *Nibbedhika Sutta* (AN 6.63) & *Cūḷakammavibhaṅga Sutta* (MN 135).
   - *Core Teaching:* Karma is **volitional action** (*Cetanā* / *Tác ý*), not fatalism. While past actions shape present conditions, present choices reshape the future.
10. **`gratitude` — Tri Ân & Báo Ân: Dấu Hiệu Của Bậc Chân Nhân**
    - *Citation:* *Kataññu Sutta* (AN 2.31–32) & Mahāyāna teaching on the *Four Heavy Graces* (*Tứ Trọng Ân*).
    - *Core Teaching:* Integrity and gratitude (*Tri ân và Báo ân*) are the hallmarks of a person of noble character (*Sappurisa*).
