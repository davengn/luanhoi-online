# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Luân Hồi Online

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
**Luân Hồi Online** is a browser-based 3D social multiplayer spiritual RPG inspired by Buddhist wisdom, East Asian and Vietnamese folk mythology, karma causality, celestial cultivation, and the Eighteen Levels of Hell.

**Core Gameplay Fantasy:**
> *"Tích công đức – Tu luyện thành tiên – Độ kiếp phi thăng – Hoặc sa vào 18 tầng địa ngục."*

Players inhabit a shared, persistent 3D world where they cultivate merit (**Công Đức**), study ethical teachings in the Wisdom Library, make moral choices that shape their net **Karma Balance** (`Công Đức - Nghiệp Lực`), ascend through 12 spiritual stages to **Thiên Đình** (Celestial Realm) via interactive **Tribulations**, or face underworld judgment in **Âm Phủ** across 18 distinct levels of Hell—each offering a meaningful path to redemption.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Shared 3D Spiritual Presence**: Inspired by online communal temples (such as Sansa and Niệm Phật Online) elevated into a full 3D WebGL/Three.js world with real-time avatar synchronization, synchronized prostrations, group meditation, and lantern festivals.
2. **Meaningful Merit & Karma Economy**: Every devotional or charitable action grants server-validated Merit with visual/audio feedback, cooldowns, and diminishing returns. Harmful choices in moral storylines accumulate **Nghiệp Lực** (Negative Karma) recorded transparently in the player's Karma Ledger.
3. **Three Interconnected Realms**:
   - **Phàm Giới (Human Realm)**: Grand Temple Courtyard, Buddha Hall, Lotus Pond, Bell Tower, Incense Altar, Prayer-Bead Pavilion, Bamboo Forest & Waterfall, Wisdom Library, and Karma Judgment Gate.
   - **Thiên Đình (Celestial Realm)**: Heavenly Gate, Cloud Palace, Celestial Garden, Immortal Pavilion, Heavenly Court, and the Tribulation Arena.
   - **Âm Phủ (Underworld & 18 Levels of Hell)**: River of Forgotten Souls, Judgment Hall, Karma Ledger, Reincarnation Gate, and 18 individually themed Hell levels with custom environments, wardens, punishment hazards, and redemption quests.
4. **Authentic & Respectful Wisdom Learning**: Interactive moral dilemmas, Dharma cards, and reflections that clearly distinguish historical Buddhist teachings (Nikāya/Āgama & Mahāyāna traditions) from regional folk mythology and fictional Xianxia cultivation ranks.
5. **Ethical Non-Pay-To-Win Economy**: Merit and Karma can never be purchased. Cosmetic items (robes, auras, nameplates, titles) are unlocked via earned **Sen** and **Tiên Ngọc** currencies.

## 3. Target Performance & Accessibility
- Smooth 60 FPS on desktop browsers and stable 30+ FPS on mobile browsers via adaptive graphics presets (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`), frustum culling, instanced foliage/particles, and on-demand resource disposal.
- Full keyboard (WASD / Arrow keys + hotkeys 1–6) and mouse/touch click-to-move + HUD controls.
